## Phone steps
1. Open the link in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Turn the phone **sideways**.
3. Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Open the Lucid icon. Clip on a Backbone if you have one.

Left stick walk · right stick look · side **DESIGN** tab to weave people / cars / things into the void. Save the dream. Come back later.
